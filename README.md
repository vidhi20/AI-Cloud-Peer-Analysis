# AI Cloud Peer Analysis: Q2 2026

An investor-relations style peer monitor of public AI cloud companies, built from earnings releases and SEC filings.

## What's inside
- `AI_Cloud_Peer_Comps_Q2_2026.xlsx`: comps model comparing CoreWeave and Nebius on revenue growth, adjusted EBITDA margin, capex intensity, backlog/ARR, and power capacity (inputs in blue, formulas in black)
- `AI_Cloud_Peer_Brief_Q2_2026.pdf` / `.docx`: one-page investor brief with key themes and implications for investor materials

## Key takeaways
| Q2 2026 | CoreWeave | Nebius |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $2.58B (+112% YoY) | $582M (+454% YoY) |
| Adjusted EBITDA margin | 59% | 41% |
| Capex / revenue | 2.5x | 9.8x |
| Contracted demand | ~$104B backlog | $3.0B ARR |

- Contracted demand (backlog, ARR, prepayments) and power secured are the metrics investors watch most.
- Strong EBITDA margins hide thin operating margins once depreciation and interest are counted.
- Funding the build-out (debt vs. customer prepayments) is a key investor question.
